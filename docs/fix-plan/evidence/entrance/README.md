# Device entrances: evidence

Each tile is rendered with `engine.renderAt(t)` and `engine.readPixels()` in `/lab`, at 1200 px wide with supersample 1, from the template's demo document. In the entrance images `loop` is turned off, because a looping doc skips shot 0's entrance (`contracts.md` §5).

- `rise-quiet-hero.jpg`: `quiet-hero` 16:9 at t = 0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.4, 0.8 and 2 s. At t = 0 nothing of the browser is drawn: no screen, toolbar, body or shadow. It then fades in as one image while rising 3% of the frame height, and has settled by 0.8 s. Before this branch, the t = 0 frame showed the browser fully drawn.
- `stagger-cascade-stack.jpg`: `cascade-stack` 16:9 at t = 0, 0.15, 0.3, 0.45, 0.6 and 1.2 s. The windows enter back to front, 0.1 s apart, each fading with a rise and a 0.97 → 1 scale. A window that is still fading shows the windows behind it through it, never its own body or toolbar.
- `stagger-responsive-pair.jpg`: `responsive-pair` 16:9 at the same times. The phone stays in front of the browser while it fades, under the orbiting camera. Before the draw order came from `LayoutNode.depthOrder`, the half-faded phone was drawn behind the browser at t = 0.3 and 0.45 s.
- `stagger-responsive-trio.jpg`: `responsive-trio` 9:16 at the same times (tiles at half size). The tablet and phone enter after the browser and stay in front of it.
- `loop-wrap.jpg` (tiles at half size): frames at total − 0.8, − 0.5, − 0.3, − 0.1 s, then 0 and 0.4 s of the looping templates. The top row is `quiet-hero` with shot 0's entrance kept in the loop. That option was rejected: the wrap crossfade fades the browser out with opacity alone into an empty canvas, which stays blank for about 0.3 s before the browser rises in again, every 5.2 s. The middle (`quiet-hero`) and bottom (`cascade-stack`) rows show what ships: a looping doc skips shot 0's entrance, so the wrap dissolves between the two camera poses as in `fix/camera-loop-seam`, and the frame just before the loop point matches frame 0.
