# S00 evidence: reference strips

Each strip is 8 frames of a Rico Stack preview (4:5, 1080 × 1350), at 0, 0.18, 0.37, 0.55, 0.73, 0.92, 1.10 and 1.28 s, left to right and top to bottom, scaled to 135 px per frame. Captured 18 October 2026 from the public preview videos; kept here only as evidence, never in the product.

- `reference-stack-2-01.png` (landscape, recommended): the front card drops out of the bottom of the frame by about 0.5 s while the three cards behind move forward one slot; a new card slides out from behind the last one.
- `reference-stack-2-02.png` (portrait, recommended): the same motion with portrait cards at our phone screen aspect.
- `reference-stack-1-01.png`: the front card drops below the deck, then rises and tucks in at the back; nothing leaves the frame (a reversal under quality bar §2.5).
- `reference-stack-3-01.png` (1.0 s loop, so 6 frames): the front card drops a little while growing, then slides back up behind the deck.
