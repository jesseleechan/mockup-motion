## Done since it was reported

- **Done ([#130](https://github.com/jesseleechan/mockup-motion/pull/130)): the editor's asset provider kept every image it decoded**, reported in #125. `createEditorAssetProvider` kept one decode per asset and width while the stage was open, so a screenshot removed from the project, and each width the stage asked for before a resize, stayed decoded; the thumbnails' provider did the same for the whole session. The provider now lends its decodes (`DecodeCache`, `src/editor/decode-cache.ts`). The engine hands each one back through the new optional `AssetProvider.releaseImage` (`contracts.md` §7) when it releases the texture that shows it (`retainOnly`, a wider or taller load replacing it, `dispose`, or a load that lands after `dispose`), and a tall page's as soon as `TextureManager` has cut it into strips; the last borrower back closes the bitmap. A decode someone still holds, such as the export's copy source, stays open, and requests at the same size share one decode, also while it decodes. In an editor session of five 1440 × 4000 uploads to a Desktop Site Tour, two of them removed, four stage resizes and three template switches, the stage's provider ends holding 2 decodes (5.8 MB) instead of 8 (52.4 MB), and the thumbnails' 3 (4.6 MB) instead of 5 (6.2 MB); a closed decode that is needed again is decoded again, 44 blob decodes over that session instead of 36. `tests/editor-asset-provider.test.ts` checks the rules with the engine's `TextureManager`. Every visual still is byte-identical.
- **Done ([#129](https://github.com/jesseleechan/mockup-motion/pull/129)): the export transferred the cached text rasters to the worker**, reported in F09 and F11 and reproduced in #123. `rasterizeText` caches the URL pill's and Before & After's label rasters for the whole page, and the video export transferred them to its worker, so the next export at the same size posted detached bitmaps and failed with `DataCloneError`: a bundle's second video, a second export, and `npm run template-previews` on two templates that show one URL. The editor's preview shares that cache. The export now sends the worker a copy of each raster, as F09 does for images (`copyForWorker`, `src/export/engine-export.ts`), and closes every copy the worker doesn't take; a failed post also stops its worker, which stayed alive before. `tests/export-transfer.test.ts` runs two exports with one provider against a stand-in worker that refuses detached bitmaps, as Chrome does. The two previews render byte-identical to `main`'s.
- **Done (Visual baselines "Render every still"): the Visual baselines workflow couldn't show that a change leaves the stills pixel-identical**, reported in #123. A run kept every committed still within the 0.2% tolerance, so a change under it came back as the old baseline. The workflow now has a **Render every still** input (`fresh`) that deletes the committed baselines first, and `tests/visual/README.md` shows how to compare a branch with `main`. The throwaway `visual-baselines/` branches the workaround needed also used up Vercel's 100 free deployments a day; `vercel.json` now skips deploying those branches.
- **Done ([#123](https://github.com/jesseleechan/mockup-motion/pull/123)): the URL pill's domain sat high in its pill, and Site Tour showed one URL for the whole tour**, reported in Presets round two T2 and T1. The pill's text now sits with its capitals and ascenders on the pill's centre line, as Before & After's labels do; `RASTER_CAP_MIDDLE_EM` and the raster's padding are shared from `src/text/metrics.ts`. A Desktop Site Tour's pill shows each page's URL: the domain from `browserUrl` plus the page's path from its capture (`pageUrl`, `src/motion/page-url.ts`), the home page as the domain alone, and a page without a capture URL as `browserUrl`. The leaving page's URL fades out over the first half of the step's eased progress and the arriving page's fades in over the second half, as the slider's window fade does (`pillUrls`, unit-tested in `tests/page-url.test.ts`); every page's URL is rasterized and uploaded in `setDocument` and goes to the export worker.
