# Quality-bar review

The [`quality-bar.md`](quality-bar.md) §8 checklist, applied to the 12 built-in templates. *On 2026-10-08 the gallery was cut to 7, then to 4; the sections for Quiet Hero, Tilted Showcase, Responsive Pair, Responsive Trio, Phone Spotlight, Phone Parade, Portfolio Rows, Isometric Wall, Cascade Stack, Launch Reel and Case Study Reel are kept as a record. Their looks live on as lab fixtures (`src/lab/layout-fixtures.ts`) for saved projects.* This review replaces the WP-18 version, which ticked every item without evidence.

**Source:** the F11 contact sheet, [`docs/fix-plan/evidence/F11/contact-sheet/`](../fix-plan/evidence/F11/contact-sheet/index.html), which you signed off on 2026-10-06 ([PR #13](https://github.com/jesseleechan/mockup-motion/pull/13)). It shows 12 templates × 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16 × 15%, 50% and 85% of each template's length: 108 stills, 960 px wide, WebP q80, rendered by `npm run contact-sheet` in Chrome on SwiftShader. F12 recaptured the demo sites after the sheet was made; the [F13 baselines](../fix-plan/evidence/F13/baselines-templates.png) show the same looks with the new captures.

**Method (F14, 2026-10-07):** I looked at all 108 stills, arranged as one 3 × 3 grid per template. On the light-background templates I measured the screen's span with a script (dark pixels against the background). I measured text contrast on the reel stills (brightest text pixel against the median of the box around it).

**Results:**

- **Pass:** seen on the stills, or proven by the linked test.
- **Partly verified:** part of the item has evidence; the note says which part doesn't.
- **Not verified:** nobody has checked it.
- **n/a:** the item doesn't apply.

The per-template tables below repeat only the items with something template-specific to say.

**Marquee speed (found after the review, from your report that demo content plays much faster than empty slots; fixed in [#18](https://github.com/jesseleechan/mockup-motion/pull/18)):** marquees used to move a whole number of periods per shot, at least one, and a period is one card per screenshot, so speed grew with the number of screenshots and exceeded §2.5's 0.12 frame widths per second. Speed now comes from the layout's speed setting alone (`src/motion/layouts/marquee.ts`), and `layouts.test.ts` "Marquee speed (quality-bar §2.5)" checks the limit for rows, columns and wall at 1–12 screenshots × 5 aspects. The marquee templates loop with the 0.8 s wrap crossfade. The figures below come from `resolveLayout` on each template's demo document (median node speed, in stage units along the layout, divided by the frame width); empty slots now move at the same speed.
