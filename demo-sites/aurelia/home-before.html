About Us

Founded in Zurich and Kyoto, Aurelia Architects approaches architecture as a slow geologic intervention. We craft structures that age into the landscape, not against it: every project balances thermal mass, deep shadow and native mineral textures to build shelter that outlives architectural fashion.
